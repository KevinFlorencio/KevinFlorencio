## About

IT graduate (Colegio EPI) with a background in HBO ICT at Fontys University of
Applied Sciences. I work mainly on the defensive side of security — standing up
and instrumenting lab environments, wiring SIEM and threat-intelligence
pipelines, and documenting the whole process end to end. I spend time on the
offensive side as well, because detections are only as good as the
understanding of what they are meant to catch.

Currently preparing for my next study programme while continuing to build and
write up hands-on projects. I also do independent security research on public
bug bounty programmes.

## Focus

My long-term goal is penetration testing. I am deliberately building breadth
first — systems administration, SOC analysis, and general IT operations —
because good offensive work depends on understanding how environments are
actually run, monitored, and defended.
